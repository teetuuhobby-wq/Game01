STRIDER HOME (a work in progress, made by a friend)
====================================================

You live on a city that walks across a desert on six giant legs. There's no fighting
yet: this early version is about the town and its people. It starts with a short
guided round (about 10 minutes): Mira asks you to help Pip with his deliveries.
Afterwards, keep exploring as long as you like.

HOW TO START
  Double-click "Strider Home.exe".
  Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" (the game isn't from a store):
  click "More info", then "Run anyway".
  If it's slow or choppy, press F6 for low quality, or start it with
  "Play (low quality).bat" instead.

CONTROLS
  Click in the window       then the mouse looks around
  WASD or arrow keys        walk          Shift   run
  E                         talk to people, read boards, sit down, look at things
  Mouse wheel or + -        zoom (all the way in = through your own eyes)
  Z X                       turn
  T                         jump an hour ahead (after the round)
  F4 / F5                   sound and light settings (have a play)
  F6                        low / high quality
  Esc                       frees the mouse; Esc again quits

Your settings are saved in your own user folder, not next to the game.
Sounds: CC0 recordings from Kenney and Freesound (see "Sound credits.txt").
