## Player-visible bugs (fix these)

**Main**
- M1. Mouse wheel over the F4/F5 panels changes the slider under the cursor instead of scrolling (`panel_base.gd` slider: `scrollable` left on). Save then keeps the accident.
- M2. Esc closes a panel and leaves the mouse free, so a second Esc quits the game at once, losing unsaved slider work (`panel_base.gd` + `main.gd` quit).
- M3. Choosing "all" for a looping sound (crowd) makes it restart on a random recording on every slider step (`sfx.gd` `refresh_loop`).
- M4. Opening F5 while F4 is open hides it behind the board (panels don't close each other). Medium confidence.
- M5. Shopkeepers and market groups snap-turn up to ~19° every ~54 s, when the city's clock loops (`people.gd` idler sway uses `t`).
- M6. Crowd recordings loop without the seam blend the other loops got (possible click each wrap; only the user can hear it).

**Town (`experiment/tidy`)**
- T1. You can talk to Sabeth, Dorran, Pell and Tamsin through the outside walls (`interact.gd`: residents skip the same-room check boards and seats have).
- T2. Two seated regulars (guild, bath) aren't on a real seat, so the seat next to them stays usable and you sit inside them.
- T3. Walking into a named resident (e.g. behind the bar) fills the screen; they're not hidden near the camera like everyone else.
- T4. Sitting in first person keeps standing eye height; sitting with the valve hides it. Cosmetic.

**Combat toys**
- A1. The beetle's charge hits up to 1.9 m from its line, but the red strip shows 1.4 m: you get hit while standing off the red (`action.gd`).
- A2. Clicks and dodges pressed during the brief hit-freeze or stagger are swallowed: combos break, rolls don't happen.
- A3. Holding Esc a bit long leaves the toy and then quits the whole program (key repeat).
- A4. Turn-based: once Bram is down, "Sand Sweep: 6 to both" hits you twice (12).
- A5. Turn-based: the burrowed beetle can still be hit; killing it then shows a win with no beetle. A KO by Erupt shows "STUCK!" over the beetle.

**Laptop speed**
- L1. F6 and F5 both set the haze: switching F6 back restores the start-up haze, not your mood setting, and any F5 change in low mode removes the extra haze that hides the shorter view.
- L2. "Hide far people" only hides the market crowd, so zoomed out in low mode the market empties while everyone else stays.
