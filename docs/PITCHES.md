# Phase 2: three game pitches (2026-10-26)

All three are built on your confirmed desires: (1) a safe walking-city home in a dangerous world, (2) everyday life in that home, (3) one big earned payoff. All three use the code-built voxel look from the look test.

---

## A. "Strider Home": live on the back of a walking city

**Hook:** You're one resident of a small walking town. Make it home, help your neighbours, and slip down to the wasteland for parts before the dust storms roll in.

**First 30 seconds:** You wake up in your little house on the deck. The whole town sways as a giant leg takes a step. A neighbour shouts that the rain-catcher broke. You fix it, then ride the cargo lift down to the sand while the city waits for you.

**The loop:** Daily life on the deck (fix, build, decorate, chat) → the city stops → explore the wasteland for parts and secrets → get back before dark → improve the city.

**The payoff:** Everyone calls your city a junk-heap backwater. Then the great threat arrives, and everything you built and everyone you helped comes together.

**Look and feel:** Town to City's cozy diorama, in the style of *A Short Hike*, with *Spiritfarer*'s "your home travels with you."

**Honest size:** First toy is **Small** (walk around the deck while it sways). A full game is **Large**, because neighbours and dialogue take lots of content.

**Desires hit:** 🏙️🏙️🏙️ ☕☕☕ ⚡⚡

---

## B. "Strider Nights": build by day, hold the walls by night

**Hook:** By day your city walks and you build. At night it has to kneel and rest, and things climb the legs.

**First 30 seconds:** Sunset. The legs fold and the city kneels into the sand. A bell rings. The first creatures are already climbing leg number three, and you grab a lantern and run for the ladder.

**The loop:** Day: choose and build rooms and defences on the deck → Night: defend while the city rests → each night is harder → each dawn, walk on.

**The payoff:** The final night. Everything you built gets tested at once.

**Look and feel:** *Kingdom Two Crowns*' day/night rhythm, Town to City's look, plus a dark wasteland lit by your city's windows.

**Honest size:** **Medium.** This is the most "game-shaped" pitch and the fastest to make fun. It has less everyday life than A.

**Desires hit:** 🏙️🏙️🏙️ ☕ ⚡⚡⚡

---

## C. "Heartwalker": *you are* the city (the one you wouldn't have picked)

**Hook:** You don't live on the walking city. You *are* it. You place each giant foot yourself, and a town grows on your back.

**First 30 seconds:** A huge, slow first step. You pick where the foot lands: on the rock, not the sinkhole. The dust clears, and tiny people on your back cheer and start building a new house.

**The loop:** Steer and step across the wasteland (puzzles: chasms, storms, monster nests) → find resources → your town grows → zoom in to see little moments of everyday life.

**The payoff:** The smallest, slowest walker in the world faces the walker-killer. It's the underdog moment, and *you are* the underdog.

**Look and feel:** *The Wandering Village*, *Shadow of the Colossus*-scale moments, the voxel look.

**Honest size:** **Medium.** The risky part is whether stepping feels good, so we'd prototype that first. Everyday life is something you watch more than live.

**Desires hit:** 🏙️🏙️🏙️ ☕ ⚡⚡⚡

---

**Claude's pick: A**, because it's the only one that hits all three desires hard. Its risk is content volume, so we'd start with B's day/night tension as the "danger," which is cheap to build and keeps it from becoming an endless cozy sim.
